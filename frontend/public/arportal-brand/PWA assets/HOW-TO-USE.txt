Atomic Portal (Client Portal) app icon - B4d

Copy the png/ files and favicon.ico into your app's public folder.

<head> tags:
  <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="any">
  <link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml" href="/b4d-favicon.svg">
  <link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">
  <meta name="theme-color" content="#07212D">

manifest.json "icons":
  "icons": [
    { "src": "/icon-192.png", "sizes": "192x192", "type": "image/png", "purpose": "any" },

Notes
- The app icons are full-bleed squares on purpose. iOS and Android cut the rounded corners themselves;
  baking corners in gives you a double-rounded edge.
- The maskable icons keep the atom inside Android's circular safe zone, so it never gets clipped.
- favicon-16 is a simplified version (just the keyhole core). The full atom turns to mush at 16px.
- b4d-app-icon-rounded.svg / b4d-rounded-1024.png are for anywhere else you want the tile on its own
  (website, docs, social).
