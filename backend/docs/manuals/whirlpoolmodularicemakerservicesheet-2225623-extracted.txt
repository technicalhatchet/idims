MODULAR ICE MAKER SERVICE SHEET — Part 2225623 (120 V models)

MODULE TEST POINTS: L, N, M, T, H, V (stamped circuit module; 3 removal screws)

SPECIFICATIONS:
- Mold heater: 185 W, 72 Ω
- Thermostat (bimetal): closes 17°F ±3°; opens 32°F ±3°
- Water fill: 140 cc, 7.5 sec
- Motor: 1.5 W, 8800 Ω
- Cycle: one revolution (ejects ice + water fill)

MODULE OHMMETER CHECKS (no power; ejector blades in PARK):
| Points | Component        | Position              | Ω     |
| L-H    | Mold heater      | Attached to support   | 72    |
| L-M    | Motor            | Disconnected support  | 8800  |

MODULE VOLTAGE CHECKS (power on; meter or test light):
| Points | Component   | Line V =        | 0 V =      |
| L-N    | Module power| Power OK        | No power   |
| T-H    | Bimetal     | Open            | Closed     |
| L-H    | Heater      | ON              | OFF        |
| L-M    | Motor       | ON              | OFF        |
| N-V    | Water valve | ON (fill)       | OFF        |

WATER LEVEL ADJUSTMENT (clockwise decreases fill):
- 1/2 turn ≈ 20 cc or 1.2 sec
- 1 full turn ≈ 40 cc or 2.4 sec
- Max one full turn either direction (module damage)

SERVICE NOTES:
- Cover: pull water adjustment knob, snap off cover; index knob on reinstall
- Module removal: Phillips through mold attachment access ports; disconnect shut-off arm
- Ejector blades: align "D" coupling with module cam on reinstall
- Do not manually start cycle — initiate electrically only
