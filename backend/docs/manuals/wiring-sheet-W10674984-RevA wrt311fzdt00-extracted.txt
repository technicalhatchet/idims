SERVICE AND WIRING SHEET — W10674984 A
Model family: WRT311FDZT00 (top-mount, ADC 2000)

ELECTRICAL SHOCK HAZARD — Disconnect power before servicing.

PERFORMANCE DATA (sealed system, normal run):
| Ambient | Watts | High side PSIG | Low side |
|---------|-------|----------------|----------|
| 70°F    | 120±20 | 120±20        | -6" to 3# |
| 90°F    | 130±20 | 160±20        | -4" to 3# |
| 110°F   | 140±20 | 220±20        | -2" to 4# |

SERVICE INFORMATION (W10674986 A):
1. Compressor stubs (suction/process) must not be interchanged.
2. Refrigerant charge must be applied to the high side only.
3. Ice maker and water valve are not original equipment on all models.
4. Caution: Ice maker cycle must be initiated electrically; do not manually start.
5. Service defrost bi-metals open at 58°F.
6. Part numbers are found on the components.

COMPONENT DATA — Embraco EM3Z (W10445423) / EM3D (W10575980):
| Component      | EM3Z        | EM3D        |
|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| Defrost heater | 350-480 W @ 120V = 42-30 Ω | same |
| Evap fan       | 1.5-2.5 W   | 1.7-3.7 W   |
| Condenser fan  | 3.1-5.1 W   | 1.6-3.6 W   |
Relay, overload, run capacitor — see part numbers on component (NOTE #6).

ELECTRONIC DEFROST CONTROL TEST MODE (bi-metal must be closed):
Option 1: Power off 30 s → thermostat OFF → power on.
Option 2: Thermostat OFF 15 s → ON 5 s (repeat 3×) → OFF.
In test mode: defrost heater on up to 18 min or until bi-metal opens. Relay click confirms entry.
WARNING: Do not overheat evaporator if bi-metal bypassed for testing.

WIRING DIAGRAM — ADC 2000 adaptive defrost control (P1–P8)

115 VAC 60 Hz service cord → ADC → loads:
- Evap fan (GN/YL, RD/WH, WH)
- Compressor (overload, PTC, run cap)
- Condenser fan
- Defrost bi-metal + heater
- Thermostat (OR)
- Refrigerator lamps, optional ice maker / water valve

VOLTAGE TEST POINTS — ADC 2000:
| Test | Expected |
|------|----------|
| P1 (BK) to P3 (RD) | 120 VAC — compressor run-time feedback input |
| P1 (BK) to P2 (PK) | 120 VAC — defrost heater run-time feedback input |
| P2 (PK) to P6 (WH) | 120 VAC — output to defrost heater when energized |
| P6 (WH) to P4 (OR) | 120 VAC — output to compressor / evap / cond fan when cooling |
| P1 (BK) to P6 (WH) | 120 VAC — constant input when plugged in |

Wire colors: BK black, WH white, RD red, BU blue, YL yellow, OR orange, BR brown, GY gray, PK pink, PU purple, TN tan.

Evap fan connector detail A: RD/WH, WH, GN/YL.
